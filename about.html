ABOUT FEDPRO

WHO WE ARE

Technical depth. Commercial focus. Energy ambition.

FEDPRO Energy Resources Limited is established to engage in energy-related businesses including oil and gas exploration and production, renewable energy and associated technical, commercial and operating activities.

Our registered office is situated in Nigeria. We are positioned to participate in asset acquisition, data acquisition and interpretation, drilling campaigns, hydrocarbon production, petroleum-product transactions and energy-sector investments.

Our purpose is to apply disciplined engineering and commercial thinking to identify, develop and monetize energy opportunities.

Our Mission

Deliver technically rigorous, commercially grounded energy solutions that improve asset performance and long-term value.

Our Approach
