CORPORATE OBJECTS

Our operating mandate.

The following summarizes the principal objects for which FEDPRO Energy Resources Limited is established.

A. Oil & Gas Exploration and Production

To act as an oil and gas industry exploration and production company, performing services including asset acquisition, data acquisition and interpretation, drilling campaigns and oil and gas production.

B. Reservoir, Production and Economics

To provide services covering production forecasting, economic evaluation, production optimization and enhancement studies, and reservoir management.

C. Petroleum Products Transactions

To transact and undertake matters incidental to petroleum products and to act as agent in procurement and marketing transactions.

D. Sales, Marketing and Storage

To carry on the business of oil and gas production, sales and marketing; to store oils, petrol, gas and kerosene; and to undertake petroleum merchandising and related infrastructure activities.

E. Additional Business Opportunities

To carry on such other businesses as the Board considers convenient and appropriate for the Company.

F. Incidental and Supporting Activities

To undertake all activities incidental or conducive to the attainment of the Company’s objects.
